# Door coverage lab

Page: `/door-coverage`, linked from Lab. All results concern prime targets p > 3
and the canonical door m0(p) = p + chi3(p), not arbitrary representations.

## Reproduce

```sh
python3 analysis/door-coverage/experiment.py
```

Python standard library only. This exhaustively sieves through 1,000,001,
uses prime targets through 1,000,000, and compares the generated census with
`src/lib/doorCoverageData.ts`. Pass `--write` to regenerate that file.

The script computes:

- First appearances of each distinct-ingredient count, including 2.
- The 36 entries for primes 5 through 43 and exponents 1, 2, 3.
- Coverage out of the 667 prime targets in 3 < p <= 5,000; first hires are
  independently searched through 1,000,000. Missing first hires are censored,
  not claimed not to exist.
- Six pairwise overlap counts in the 5,000 and 1,000,000 windows.
- The frequency of doors with at least two distinct odd ingredients.
- Finite checks of both directions of the two-class lemma and gap residues.
- A Proth proof of N = 49*2^594 + 1 (181 digits), witness 3, and supplementary
  Pocklington checks with witnesses 3 for q=2 and 2 for q=7.

The proof conditions are exact modular computations. There is no reliance on
PRP labels, floating-point primality, or unproved factor leaves. The two leaf
primes in the large example are 2 and 7. No Lean formalisation is claimed.

## Explorer

`src/lib/doorCoverage.ts` supplies a separate small exact sieve through 12,001.
The visible range ends at 10,000 at most, leaving lookahead for the next hire.
The top lane includes all primes (including 2 and 3); ingredient lanes use
odd prime targets >3. Composite candidates are included for inspection but do
not count as members of a coverage set.

Each lane Q is generated by even doors d divisible by Q with 3 not dividing d.
Its candidate is d+1 when d=1 mod 3, and d-1 when d=2 mod 3. The matching
positions are exact; gaps between successful hires are measured separately.
Shading alternates only to distinguish successive intervals and encodes no
additional category. Labels show the full gap even when a window clips it.

## Exact statements and limits

For odd Q>1 with gcd(Q,3)=1, s=chi3(Q):

    Q | m0(p) iff p = 2Q-s or 4Q+s (mod 6Q), for prime p>3.

The two classes are reduced modulo 6Q. The prime number theorem for arithmetic
progressions therefore gives relative density 2/phi(6Q)=1/phi(Q) among primes.
This is a fixed-Q limit, not a uniform estimate for Q growing with the window.
It gives no bound on first hires or individual gaps.

Coverage intersections satisfy C_A intersect C_B = C_lcm(A,B). Positive
differences between members of C_Q lie in 0, plus/minus(2Q+2s) modulo 6Q.
These are standard congruence consequences, presented in the door language.

The page contains the elementary proof. Finite script checks support the
implementation but do not replace that proof or the classical density theorem.

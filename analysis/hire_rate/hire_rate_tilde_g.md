# Hire-rate tilde_g: zero-deflation scale, Var/μ, occupancy

## Setup

- Extends `hire_rate_multiplicity.c` → `hire_rate_tilde_g.c`: same rem-sieve
  `N_q` counts on λ-bins `0.2·2^{k/4}` over `[0.2,8]`.
- Per bin additionally: second moment → `Var(N)/μ`, occupancy `P(N=0,1,2,≥3)`.
- `under = e^{-μ_emp} − P0_emp`, `tilde_g = (log X)·under`.
- Also `g = e^{-λ_pred} − P0_emp` and `(log X)·g` for comparison.
- Checkpoints: `X ∈ {10^7, 10^8, 10^9, 10^{10}, 2·10^{10}}`.
- Wall: **1043 s** through `2·10^{10}` (RSS ~2.2 GB); in-band sat_cells=0.

## Room takeaway (three diagnosis questions)

### 1. Does `tilde_g` collapse across X on the frontier `[0.5,4]`?

**Yes — strongly.** Cosine of the 12-bin `tilde_g` vector vs `X=2·10^{10}` is
**≥ 0.996 already at `10^7`** and **≥ 0.999 for `X ≥ 10^8`**.
Raw `g` collapses much more slowly (only ~0.99 at `10^{10}`).
So the natural scale of the zero-deficit is `1/log X`:
`under ≈ tilde_g(λ) / log X` with a stable shape `tilde_g(λ)` on the frontier.

| X | cosine `tilde_g` | cosine `g` |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $10^7$ | 0.9965 | 0.8467 |
| $10^8$ | 0.9993 | 0.8345 |
| $10^9$ | 0.9995 | 0.9441 |
| $10^{10}$ | 0.9999 | 0.9924 |
| $2·10^{10}$ | 1.0000 | 1.0000 |

Peak `tilde_g ≈ 0.95–1.0` near `λ ∼ 1–1.3`; falls to ~0.3 near `λ ∼ 3.5`.
Absolute bin values for `X ≥ 10^9` typically agree within ~0.03–0.05.

### 2. Is `Var/μ < 1` (true under-dispersion) or ≈1 with only zero-deflation?

**True under-dispersion: `Var(N)/μ ∈ [0.66, 0.83]` on the frontier**, clearly below 1.
Not a pure zero-inflation/deflation tweak of a Poisson (that would keep `Var ≈ μ`).
Ratio drifts slowly upward with X (mean ~0.71 → 0.79 from `10^7` → `2·10^{10}`) but
remains well below 1 at the largest X.

| X | min | median | max | nq-weighted mean `[0.5,4)` |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $10^7$ | 0.6578 | 0.7196 | 0.7964 | 0.7349 |
| $10^8$ | 0.6914 | 0.7467 | 0.7973 | 0.7577 |
| $10^9$ | 0.7156 | 0.7688 | 0.8214 | 0.7801 |
| $10^{10}$ | 0.7428 | 0.7905 | 0.8226 | 0.7978 |
| $2·10^{10}$ | 0.7499 | 0.7910 | 0.8278 | 0.8027 |

### 3. Does occupancy look Poisson-binomial / Bernoulli-sum vs Poisson?

**Poisson-binomial-like, not Poisson(μ).** Relative to `Poisson(μ_emp)` at `X=2·10^{10}`:
- **fewer zeros** (`P0_emp < e^{-μ}`),
- **more singles** at low–mid λ (`P1_emp > μ e^{-μ}`),
- **fewer high counts** at low–mid λ (`P(N≥3)_emp < Poisson`),
- together with `Var < μ` — the classic signature of a sum of independent
  heterogeneous Bernoullis (owner indicators), not an equidispersed Poisson.

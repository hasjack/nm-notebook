# Hire-rate multiplicity: N_q vs Poisson on the λ-frontier

## Setup

- For each odd prime `q ≠ 3` with `λ = Li(X)/(q−1) ∈ [0.2, 8]`, count
  `N_q(X) = #{prime owners p ≤ X : q | m_0(p)}` (rem-sieve factorisation).
- Dense `uint8` count band on the union of those q-ranges (no hired bitset).
- λ-bins: 22 log edges `0.2 · 2^{k/4}` up to 8.
- Per bin: `μ_emp = mean N_q`, `λ_pred = mean Li/(q−1)`, `P0_emp = fraction N_q=0`,
  `g = e^{-λ_pred} − P0_emp = P_emp(N>0) − (1−e^{-λ})`.
- Decomposition: `g = (e^{-λ}−e^{-μ}) + (e^{-μ}−P0) = mean_part + under_part`.
- Checkpoints: `X ∈ {10^7, 10^8, 10^9, 10^{10}, 2·10^{10}}`.
- Wall: ~491 s through `10^{10}` (RSS ~1.1 GB); ~1046 s through `2·10^{10}` (RSS ~2.2 GB).
- `uint8` saturation hits only out-of-band small-q (λ ≫ 8 at large X); in-band `μ ≈ λ` to ~0.05.

## Room takeaway

**Residual is under-dispersion of zeros, not mean-bias.**
Across frontier bins `λ ∈ [0.5, 4]` and all listed X:
1. **`under_part = e^{-μ} − P0 > 0`** in every fine bin (fewer zeros than Poisson(μ)).
2. **`bias = μ − λ` is typically negative** (μ slightly below λ) — mean-bias goes the *wrong* way for an excess-hire residual.
3. **`g > 0`** in the bulk of `[0.67, 4)`: under-dispersion is strong enough to overcome the slight mean deficit,
   producing the same ~3–5% excess hire rate previously seen as `H − P_Pois` on these λ-bins.
4. **`g_X(λ)` collapses across X**: cosine vs `X = 2·10^{10}` is ≥ 0.94 for `X ≥ 10^9` and **0.998** at `10^{10}`.

So the frontier residual is **under-zero-Poisson** (left-tail thin), **not** `μ_emp > λ`.

| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $10^7$ | 58135 | 56363 | 1772 | 3.14% |

| $10^9$ | 3386835 | 3248882 | 137953 | 4.25% |

Relative excess ~3.7–4.2% matches the prior λ-bin residual profile on `[0.5,4)` (~2–5% by sub-bin).

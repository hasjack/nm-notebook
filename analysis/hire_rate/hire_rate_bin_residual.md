# Hire-rate bin residual H − P_Pois

## Setup

- Hired odd primes `q ≠ 3` by owners `p ≤ X` (rem-sieve bitset; same core as `hire_rate_HX_core`).
- Poisson prediction per prime: `p_hire = 1 − exp(−Li(X)/q)` with `Li(X) = Ei(ln X)` (scipy-matched).
- Population for bins: odd primes `5 ≤ q ≤ X/2` (matches hire set; excluding `{2,3}` changes ΣP by ~2).
- `u = log(q)/log(X)`; 10 equal bins on `[0,1)`. Also decades `[10^k, 10^{k+1})`.
- Residual = `H_bin − P_bin`; `rel = residual/P_bin`.
- Full enumeration through `X = 2·10^10` (wall ≈ 1185 s, peak RSS ≈ 650 MB). `H_gt_half = 0` at all checkpoints (largest hireable `q` is `≤ (X+1)/2`).

## Answer: where does the ~1.4% excess live?

**Concentrated at large `u`, not uniform, and not at the extreme boundary.**

- For `u ≤ 0.7`: residual ≈ 0 (near-certain hire; `H ≈ P ≈ π` in-bin).
- At `X ≥ 10^9`: **≥95% of the positive residual sits in `u ∈ [0.8, 0.9)`** (`q ∈ [X^{0.8}, X^{0.9})`).
- The top bin `u ∈ [0.9, 1.0]` (near `q ~ X/2`) flips from a positive contribution at `X ≤ 10^8` to **near-zero / negative** at `X ≥ 10^10` (Poisson slightly over-predicts the extreme tail).
- Decade view agrees: at `X = 10^10` the excess is in `[10^8, 10^9)` (+1.19M, rel ≈ +4.3%); `[10^9, 5·10^9]` is a deficit (−0.24M).
- So the global `R_Pois − 1 ≈ 1.4%` at `X = 10^10` is a **mid-to-large scale** effect (`u ~ 0.85`), not a small-`q` effect and not an edge-`u→1` pile-up.

The `2q`-truncated model **under-predicts** badly (`R_trunc ≈ 1.38`): it zeros out too much mass for `q` near `X/2` (`Li(2q) ≈ Li(X)`), where many hires still occur via owners `p ∼ q`. Bin-wise, almost all of the trunc residual sits in `u ∈ [0.9, 1.0]`. Truncation at `2q` is not a good correction for the 1.4% excess.

## Method notes

- Binary: `drafts/hire_rate_bin_residual` (from `hire_rate_bin_residual.c`).
- Single rem-sieve pass to `X_max = 2e10`; at each checkpoint, segmented prime scan to `X/2` against the hired bitset.
- Ei fixed with power series through `x ≤ 40` and optimally truncated asymptotic beyond (needed for `Li(2q)` in the trunc model).
- Papers frozen; no Mathlib; no git push.
